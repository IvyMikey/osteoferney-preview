Osteopath in Ferney-Voltaire

Pain in the back, the neck, the joints.

Thomas Garnier, osteopath D.O., sees you for a session that begins with a conversation and an examination, then gentle treatment adapted to your situation.

Adults, people who play sport, pregnant women, infants and older adults.

The practice is in Ferney-Voltaire, in the Pays de Gex, and also sees patients who come from Geneva.

Your osteopath

A graduate of the Collège Ostéopathique Européen. Little or no forceful manipulation, and time to explain what the pain is expressing.
