Before you come

A consultation lasts 45 minutes on average, sometimes up to an hour. That covers the conversation and the examination, not only the time on the table.

Wear something flexible, so the joints and the movement can be examined.

No prescription is required. An osteopath is a first-contact practitioner: you book directly.

The practice is at the Centre commercial d’Aumard, in Ferney-Voltaire. By car, Parking du Bijou is a one-minute walk. By bus, the Ferney Centre stop (lines F and 66) is about two minutes away.

Fee and payment

Osteopathy session: €70. K-tape application: €35, in a 15-minute slot that you request by message.

Payment by cash, cheque or bank transfer. Bank cards are not accepted.

French national health insurance does not cover osteopathy. Many complementary insurers reimburse all or part of the session, depending on your contract, when you send the invoice.

Urgent appointments

I keep slots each day for urgent care: acute low-back pain, a stiff neck, and similar situations. Look at today’s openings on Doctolib, or leave a voicemail, an SMS or a WhatsApp message. I do not pick up the phone during a consultation.

If you are still unsure

That is a fair question. People come for the back, the neck, the joints, headaches, stress held in the body, or care during pregnancy. You can read the reasons to come and the reviews before you decide.
