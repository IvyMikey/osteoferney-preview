When to come

When should you see an osteopath?

You may already have consulted without really feeling listened to, or while dreading manipulation that felt too abrupt.

My osteopathic approach, in Ferney-Voltaire, is meant to be different: gentle, reassuring, and turned toward your actual needs, so that each session restores your confidence in your body and in your capacity to get better.

Read about the approach

When to consult: the criteria

The main reasons to book a consultation are that your pain:

01

They limit you

In your daily activities, or in sport.

02

They worry you

Your pain worries you.

03

They persist

They continue over time.

Spinal pain

Low-back pain (lombalgies): pain after prolonged sitting, effort or stress.

Neck pain (cervicalgies): tension, stiffness, sometimes together with headaches.

Mid-back pain (dorsalgies): tension and stiffness that limit you in daily life.

Osteopathy can help reduce the pain, improve mobility, and restore your confidence in movement.

Joints and muscles

Shoulders

Tendinopathy, stiffness, discomfort when lifting the arm.

Knees and ankles

Pain when walking, after a sprain, or with sport.

Wrists and elbows

Repetitive movements, computer work, sport.

Adapted to your activity

Each treatment is adapted to your activity (sport, work, daily life) so that you can recover those movements without pain.

Headaches and the physical side of stress

Tension headaches, migraine, a clenched jaw, facial pain, muscular fatigue linked to stress or poor sleep.

Osteopathy does not treat stress itself. It can reduce the physical consequences, and help the body recover.

Pregnancy, infants, older adults

The practice sees every age. Pregnant women come for the discomforts of pregnancy. Infants come for a check-up or a specific concern. Older adults come for mobility, a pain that has settled in, or simply to feel steadier in their movements.

Consult your osteopath in safety

Your osteopath is trained to recognise situations that constitute a medical emergency, and those in which a consultation with a doctor is required, and will refer you on (for example, a suspected fracture).

A session centred on your pain

Every pain has a history. In consultation, we look together for an understanding of yours, to help you recover comfort, mobility and confidence in your body.

Working with the body

With gentle techniques drawn from functional osteopathy and from pain neuroscience, I help your body recover its reference points in movement.

Giving meaning to the pain

Understanding the mechanisms of pain is how you regain some authority over it. You come to see that your body is not “broken”, but protective and able to adapt.

Becoming more autonomous

You leave with concrete tools to use yourself, according to your needs, in order to extend the effects of the session and take an active part in your health.
