Your osteopath

Thomas Garnier

Thomas Garnier, your osteopath D.O. in Ferney-Voltaire, offers a gentle, personal approach to ease your pain and improve your mobility.

A D.O. graduate of the Collège Ostéopathique Européen, trained in the neuroscience of pain.

You have pain in the back, the neck or the joints, and you are looking for a lasting solution. As an osteopath in Ferney-Voltaire, I accompany my patients with a gentle, global approach, centred on listening and education, to help you understand your pain and regain confidence in your body.

See the main reasons for a consultation

Many patients hesitate to consult, for fear of abrupt manipulation

My approach is different:

  • little or no high-velocity manipulation (the “cracks”);
  • techniques adapted to your comfort;
  • follow-up that respects your pace and your needs.

Each technique is chosen to ease you safely, without apprehension.

Osteopathy: understanding your pain in order to act

Pain is often experienced as inevitable. Yet it has meaning: it has set in within a context, and within a coherent system, your body.

My role is to help you interpret what your body is expressing, so that you can act on your pain and take an active role in your health.

You leave not only relieved, but also with a clear explanation and practical advice.

Your osteopath’s aim: to restore confidence and autonomy

An osteopathy session is not limited to the treatment itself. I take the time to pass on:

01

The mechanisms

An explanation of the mechanisms involved.

02

The exercises

Exercises suited to you, and how to bring them into daily life.

03

Recurrence

Strategies to prevent the pain from returning.

The aim: that you can manage your pain on your own, and return to your activities with confidence.

A global view of health

Your pain does not exist in isolation. It is linked to your history, your way of life, and sometimes to stress. In consultation I take all of these factors into account, in order to offer care that is coherent, personal, and directed toward the long term.
